# Getting Started
<desktop>This window contains information about everything in the game.

You can move windows around by dragging and dropping the gray part of the window.
You can pan the camera around by dragging anywhere outside the windows.

To get started with programming, go to the [First Program](docs/first_program.md) page by clicking the link.

Be sure to check out the buttons in the top right corner of the screen. Hover over them to see a tooltip explaining what they do. One of them opens the tech tree!
</desktop>
<mobile>This window contains information about everything in the game.

Windows open filling the whole screen. The button in the window's header shrinks it back to a small window and makes it fill the screen again. You can move small windows around by dragging the gray part of the window.
You can pan the camera around by dragging anywhere outside the windows and zoom with two fingers.

To get started with programming, go to the [First Program](docs/first_program.md) page by tapping the link.

Be sure to check out the buttons along the bottom edge of the screen, or in the top right corner when you hold your device sideways. Try them out to see what they do. One of them opens the tech tree!
</mobile>
