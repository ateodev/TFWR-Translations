# Petrified Pumpkins

Apparently you can find pumpkins underground. They have turned solid and become petrified, but are still good enough for our purposes.

Petrified pumpkins occur underground in blocks of 3x3x3 or 5x5x5. There is no surefire way to detect them, so you just have to hope your drone digs into one, but they occur in the hard dirt under the stone-and-iron layer, about the same height where quartz occurs (if you have it unlocked).

You might even find the odd quartz vein piercing a petrified pumpkin! This doesn't make the pumpkin more valuable or anything, but it might make you feel good to know that you've spotted a rare sight.

`if get_ground_type() == Grounds.Petrified_Pumpkin:
    do_a_flip()
`
